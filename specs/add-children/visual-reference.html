Axiom: Add Children Suggestions, visual reference

A rendered specimen of every component state on the Suggestions Surface, in both themes at once. It is a picture, not a specification.

Status: Descriptive, non normative. Owns no requirement prefix.
Specifies: nothing. The normative document is README.md.
Reference implementation: ...\projects\dd6f8408-986c-4b38-9c30-3fe336496345\index.html, read only.

Interaction states are rendered through explicit state classes rather than live pseudo classes, so that every state of a control is visible at once without a pointer. In the product these are :hover, :focus-visible, :checked, :indeterminate and :disabled. Nothing on this page is interactive, and nothing on this page is a requirement.

1. Status pill

SUG-30, SUG-31, SUG-75

Withdraw the word, which happens below 680px, and the shape plus the accessible name still carry it. The dashed border on Available is the third cue and marks it as the only actionable state.

SUG-11, SUG-46

6. The overlay panels

SUG-54 to SUG-62

Analysis and Context are reference material, consulted occasionally and never needed at the same time as the list. As overlays they cost nothing but their trigger when closed, and displace nothing when open. Both are shown open here; in the product at most one is open at a time.

Light, Analysis open
Analysis

Why Claude proposed these

Fashion merchandising is the business side of getting apparel and accessories from concept to the customer, so its natural subtypes are the functional specialisms taught as course subjects: buying, planning, retailing, presentation, promotion, and sourcing.

The topic currently has only one recorded type, which reads as a combined degree program rather than a functional area, so the suggestions sit alongside it without overlapping it.

8. The vertical budget

SUG-16, SUG-17, SUG-18, SUG-21, SUG-55

The reason SUG-18 exists. The earlier arrangement carried seven bands above the list, two of which restated values shown elsewhere and two of which were disclosures that displaced the list when opened. The arrangement below carries four, and the disclosures became overlays. Each height is the arithmetic of declared constants: band padding of 8px top and bottom, plus the tallest control in the band, plus a 1px separator.

The counts that the title band carried are now carried by the filter chips, which were already showing them. The Run timestamp and assistant that the meta line carried are now carried by the Run chooser, which was already showing them. Nothing was lost; two duplications and two displacements were removed. The Analysis preview described by SUG-21 recovers the one thing the accordions gave for free, a glimpse of the reasoning, by putting it in horizontal space that was empty anyway.

10. Responsive surrender order

SUG-79, SUG-80, SUG-81

Width is surrendered in a fixed order, and the band count never increases as it is. No essential action is withdrawn at any step: selection, commit, filter, search and expansion remain available at every width down to 375px.
